Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 7, 2026

Introduction

FiCore Academy is committed to protecting your personal and professional data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, and safeguard your structural metrics, account information, and performance indicators across our learning ecosystem.

Information We Collect

  • Account Profiling Data: Professional names, corporate or student email coordinates, and authentication tokens provided during registration.
  • Velocity & Execution Metrics: Granular performance track progress, syllabus timeline completions, active Pacing Engine durations, and diagnostic test selections.
  • Technical Identifiers: Secure browser headers, device parameters, and tokenized session IP allocations compiled for audit logging and performance security.

How We Use Your Information

  • To manage personalized dashboards, maintain study frameworks, and power diagnostic optimization engines.
  • To compile aggregated, non-identifiable transparency analytics linking cohort mastery metrics directly to verifying corporate sponsors.
  • To dispatch administrative account alerts, syllabus updates, and corporate partner notifications.

Information Sharing

FiCore Academy does not rent, sell, or disclose proprietary user information to outside third-party entities. Selected operational datasets are shared exclusively with secure cloud engines and database interfaces strictly required to execute core platform operations.

Data Security

We maintain rigorous defense controls including server-side password hashing, tokenized cross-site request forgery (CSRF) tracking layers, and isolation matrices to prevent unauthorized data manipulation or exposure.

Your Rights & Management

Users retain full authority to request access to, edit, or purge their historical performance metadata. For inquiries regarding data protection structures, profile extractions, or security logs, please launch an escalations ticket through our official team contact channels.
